Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 14, 2026

Short version: CashCalc does not collect, store, or share any personal information. All your data stays on your device.

1. Who We Are

CashCalc is a free denomination calculator app for Indian currency, developed by Lohitkumar D. The app is available as a Progressive Web App (PWA) at lohitkumard.github.io/Cash-info and as an Android app.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store any of the following:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Financial data or transaction records
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Browser history or cookies from other sites

3. Data Stored on Your Device

CashCalc stores the following data locally on your device only, using your browser's localStorage:

  • Saved cash count records — up to 50 records you choose to save, including the denomination breakdown, total amount, payee name (if entered), and timestamp. This data never leaves your device.
  • PWA install preference — a flag indicating whether the install prompt has been shown.

This data is stored in your browser/app storage and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser's site data or using the "Clear All" button in the History tab.

4. Share Functionality

CashCalc includes a "Share" feature that allows you to share a cash count summary. This feature:

  • Only activates when you explicitly tap the Share button
  • Uses your device's native sharing system (Android Share Sheet / iOS Share Sheet)
  • Does not transmit any data to our servers
  • The recipient of the shared content is entirely your choice

5. No Backend Servers

CashCalc has no backend server, no database, and no API endpoints. The app runs entirely in your browser or as an Android TWA (Trusted Web Activity). No data is ever sent over the internet as part of the app's normal operation.

6. Third-Party Services

The app is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect server-side access logs (IP address, browser type, page visited) as part of their standard hosting operations. This is governed by GitHub's Privacy Policy.

We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any other tracking services.

7. Permissions (Android App)

The Android version of CashCalc requests minimal permissions:

  • Internet — Required to load the web app on first use and check for updates
  • Vibration — Used for haptic feedback on button taps (optional, controlled by device settings)

The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location, files, or any other sensitive permissions.

8. Children's Privacy

CashCalc is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. Since we collect no personal information from any users, there is no specific risk to children's data.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
